# Chai v0.1.15

Chai now remembers which WhatsApp and Gmail messages you've read and clears
unread badges when you catch up in another app.

## Changes

- Opening a conversation clears the unread badge in Chai for the messages you
  view. Reading in Chai sends no WhatsApp read receipts and leaves Gmail's read
  status alone. Chai remembers your local reads across restarts.
- WhatsApp reads from your phone or another linked client clear badges when
  Chai receives the read update. Gmail checks for read changes at startup, when
  you return to the Chai window, and once a minute while connected. That includes
  older email threads already in Chai.
- New messages in a conversation open in the background stay unread until you
  view them.

## Install and update

Quit Chai and download the DMG for your Mac (arm64 for Apple silicon, x64 for
Intel) or the Windows x64 installer from this release. Install over your current
copy to keep your conversations, clusters, and account connections. Chai updates
its local database when you first open the new version.

These builds are unsigned. On macOS, you may need to use **Open Anyway** in
System Settings under **Privacy & Security** after the first launch attempt.
[Apple's macOS instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

If Windows shows a SmartScreen prompt, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
